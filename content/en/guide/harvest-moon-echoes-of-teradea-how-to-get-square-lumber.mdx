## The Full Square Lumber Chain, Step by Step

Most players searching Harvest Moon: Echoes of Teradea how to get square lumber assume there is one secret trick. There is not — there is a chain, and every link has to be working. Break one link and you will stand in front of a blueprint with a full inventory and nothing to hand over.

### Step 1: Get an axe and keep upgrading it

Your axe is the single most important tool in this process. Early tiers handle soft, young trees, but stumps and denser wood will shrug off a starter axe. Upgrade at the blacksmith or workshop as soon as the materials allow, and treat each upgrade as a direct lumber-rate increase. A better axe means fewer swings per tree, which means more trees per in-game day.

### Step 2: Chop mature trees, not saplings

Mature trees give the most usable timber. Saplings and young trees give you very little and remove your future supply, so leave them standing. If your farm has a wooded corner, work it in rotation instead of clearing it in one afternoon — that way the area keeps producing while you focus on other chores.

### Step 3: Turn logs into boards

Raw logs are not the same as square lumber. You need to process them, usually at a sawmill, carpenter's shop, or crafting bench, depending on how far your story has progressed. This is the step where most guides get vague, because the exact menu label can shift between patches and regional releases. Check your own crafting list and look for the board recipe that consumes lumber or logs.

### Step 4: Buy, trade, or request boards when you are short

When a blueprint deadline is close, buying finished boards is a legitimate shortcut. Shops, traveling merchants, and villager requests sometimes offer wood materials. It is rarely the cheapest route, but it is almost always faster than waiting for regrowth. Community reports suggest keeping a small cash reserve specifically for lumber emergencies.

## FAQ: Square Lumber in Echoes of Teradea

**How do I get square lumber in Harvest Moon: Echoes of Teradea?**

Fell mature trees with an upgraded axe to collect logs, then process those logs at a sawmill, carpenter's shop, or crafting bench to produce finished boards. You can also buy or trade for boards when you are short on time. The exact menu names can vary between patches, so confirm the recipe in your own crafting list.

**Do I need to upgrade my axe to make square lumber?**

Not strictly to make it, but you will need upgrades to gather wood efficiently. A starter axe struggles with denser trees and stumps, which slows your entire supply chain. Upgrading the axe is usually the single biggest time saver in the process.

**Can I buy square lumber instead of crafting it?**

Yes, in most cases. Shops, traveling merchants, and villager requests occasionally offer wood materials. Buying is faster but more expensive, so treat it as an emergency option rather than your main source.

**How much square lumber should I stockpile?**

There is no universal number, since requirements scale with your farm's progress. A safe approach is to keep a rolling buffer — enough boards to cover your next planned build plus a small surplus — and top it up whenever you have spare stamina at the end of a day.
