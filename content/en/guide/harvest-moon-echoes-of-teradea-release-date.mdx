## Day-One Guide: How to Start Strong in Teradea

The best way to honor the Harvest Moon: Echoes of Teradea release date is to start with a plan instead of wandering. Farming sims reward efficiency early, and your first in-game week sets the pace for the whole first season.

### Your First Week, Step by Step

| Priority | What to Do | Why It Pays Off |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Clear your field | Remove weeds and debris near your farmhouse | Frees up planting space immediately |
| 2. Plant fast crops | Choose short-growth crops first | Quick cash to reinvest in seeds and tools |
| 3. Meet the townsfolk | Talk to every resident you can find | Unlocks requests, tips, and romance routes |
| 4. Unlock traversal | Practice jumping and ladder climbing early | Makes later exploration much faster |
| 5. Befriend a companion | Get a pet or mount as soon as one is available | Travel and field work get easier |
| 6. Scout the wilderness | Explore edges of the map carefully | Find treasure and rare animals before you need them |

A simple rule for beginners: spend your first in-game week on seeds and stamina recovery, not on luxury items. Money compounds in farming sims, and an early investment in crops usually outperforms a cosmetic purchase.

### Wildlife Hazards and Exploration Safety

The wilderness of Teradea is not decorative. Certain areas are home to wolves, bears, and tigers, and getting caught by one can cost you items and send you straight back to the farm. Treat long expeditions like a risk-reward decision.

| Hazard | Risk | Smart Response |
|---|---|---|
| Wolves | Lost items, forced return home | Travel in daylight and keep escape routes open |
| Bears | Same as above, in rougher terrain | Avoid heavy inventory when crossing their territory |
| Tigers | High-risk zones further from the farm | Save progress mentally: bank items before you push deeper |

If you are heading out for treasure or rare animals, empty your inventory of anything you cannot afford to lose. That single habit prevents most of the frustration players report with dangerous wildlife.
