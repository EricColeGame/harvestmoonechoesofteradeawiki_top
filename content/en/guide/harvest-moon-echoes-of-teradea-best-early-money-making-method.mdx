### Why Foraging Beats Farming on Day One

Farming pays better over a full season, but it pays nothing today. Foraging pays today. Wild harvest nodes, shore pickups, and map-edge gathering spots cost no seeds, no watering, and no risk — and they refill on a timer you can learn within a week.

Sell the cheap, common forage early. Hold anything rare, because those items typically convert into better value as gifts, cooking ingredients, or request turn-ins. That single habit separates players who are broke on day ten from players who already own a tool upgrade.

## Crop Math: The Only Formula That Matters

New players rank crops by sell price. Experienced players rank them by profit per day. A high-value crop that takes most of a season to mature can lose to a cheap crop that finishes three times before the season ends.

| Metric | Why it matters | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Seed cost | Sets your break-even point | Subtract it before celebrating a sale |
| Growth days | Determines harvests per season | Fewer days means faster reinvestment |
| Regrowth | Changes long-run value completely | Check whether the crop keeps producing |
| Sell price | Raw value per unit | Compare per-unit, not per-harvest |
| Profit per day | The fair ranking metric | (Sell price − seed cost) ÷ growth days |
| Regrowing profit per day | Best case for repeat harvests | (Sell price × harvests) ÷ total days |

Run this math yourself with your own shop prices. When a new patch adjusts a value, your spreadsheet updates in seconds — and you will know instantly whether your spring planting plan still holds.

## Five Early Money Mistakes That Cost You a Season

- **Selling building materials.** Wood, stone, and ore feel like free money until you need them for restoration and upgrades.
- **Buying expensive seeds first.** Long growth times lock your gold in the ground while cheap fast crops keep cycling.
- **Ignoring village requests.** These typically pay in gold plus unlocks, which is value you cannot buy.
- **Letting stamina expire.** Unspent stamina at bedtime is wasted income potential.
- **Exploring the mist too early.** A knockout costs you a day, your energy, and sometimes items. Player experience suggests earning first and exploring second.
