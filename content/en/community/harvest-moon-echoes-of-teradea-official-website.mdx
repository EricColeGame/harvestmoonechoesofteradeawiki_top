### Farming, Romance, and the Happilia Loop

Between travels, you cultivate your farm, raise animals, harvest crops, and build a place to call home. The Happilia system ties all of that generosity together: the more you help villagers and complete requests, the more the Harvest Goddess can restore the land. In practice, that means side quests are not filler — they feed directly into world progression.

Romance runs parallel to that loop. Ten candidates is a generous roster for the series, and the listing promises meaningful relationships and heartfelt events rather than a simple gift-grinding mechanic.

### Exploration, Animal Companions, and the Wild

Traversal is the biggest departure from the classic formula. Jumping, ladder climbing, and vine scaling combine with companion abilities, so a cliff you can't reach alone may open up once the right animal joins you. Keep these habits in mind:

- Bring a companion whose ability matches the terrain you're exploring.
- Bank your inventory before entering wilderness zones — getting caught costs you collected items.
- Treat nautical charts as priority pickups if you want rare animals and hidden treasure.
- Revisit old areas after unlocking new movement skills; the map is designed to reward backtracking.
