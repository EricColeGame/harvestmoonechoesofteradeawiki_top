## How Carol and Robert Reshape Your Romance Options

This is the detail that has generated the most discussion. Carol and Robert are the two protagonist options you choose between at the start of the game. Whichever one you do not select becomes an eligible romance candidate, meaning the opposite-gender protagonist is always part of your dating pool.

| Your Protagonist | The Other Protagonist Becomes | Practical Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Carol | Robert, an eligible candidate | You can court Robert through the normal romance system |
| Robert | Carol, an eligible candidate | You can court Carol through the normal romance system |

Community reports from the reveal thread suggest players have mixed feelings about the arrangement. One recurring concern is roster math: if one of your ten options is the protagonist you did not pick, some players feel they effectively have one fewer "new" character to meet than they did in previous titles. Others see it as a bonus — a built-in childhood-friend romance that already has shared history baked in.

Either way, the practical takeaway is simple: decide early. Your protagonist choice is locked in before the story begins, and it directly determines which of the Harvest Moon Echoes of Teradea bachelors and bachelorettes will be waiting at the farm gate.

### Actionable Romance Tips That Apply to the Whole Cast

- **Learn schedules before you buy gifts.** Every candidate in a Harvest Moon game has a routine. Follow them for a few in-game days and note where they stand at each hour.
- **Match gifts to their story, not their stereotype.** Lorelei's secret at Quarrytop Mine or Sergei's fog research gives you thematic gift ideas that feel earned rather than random.
- **Bank a stack of universal favorites early.** Neutral-but-liked items keep friendship points ticking upward on days when you cannot find the perfect present.
- **Do not ignore festivals.** Seasonal events are usually the cheapest way to add heart points, and they double as free character development.
- **Court two or three candidates at once in the early game.** Commitment in most Harvest Moon titles is a deliberate late step, so exploring costs you nothing but a little time.

| Romance Stage | What to Focus On | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Early game | Daily greetings and schedule mapping | Builds baseline friendship before hearts appear |
| Mid game | Themed gifts tied to their backstory | Faster heart growth and unique dialogue |
| Seasonal | Festivals and town events | Bonus affection plus story context |
| Late game | Heart events and confession | Locks in the relationship and unlocks marriage path |

## FAQ: Harvest Moon Echoes of Teradea Bachelors and Bachelorettes

**Who are all the romance candidates in Harvest Moon: Echoes of Teradea?**

Ten candidates have been revealed: Carol, Robert, Sergei, Lorelei, Shirley, Bryce, Aaron, Jane, Hank, and Mara. Each one has a distinct background, from Sergei's fog research to Mara's sailing ambitions.

**Can you romance the other protagonist?**

Yes. Carol and Robert are the two playable protagonist options, and whichever one you do not choose becomes an eligible romance candidate in your save file. Your starting choice effectively decides which of the two you can court.

**Is same-sex marriage available in the game?**

Natsume has not circulated a clear, definitive answer yet. Players have asked directly in community threads, and the reply so far has been inconclusive, so it is best to wait for an official statement rather than assume either way.

**When does Harvest Moon: Echoes of Teradea release, and on what platforms?**

The game is scheduled for October 15, 2026, and is listed for Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC.

**How many romance candidates will I actually have in one playthrough?**

You will see ten named candidates, but only one of them — the protagonist you did not pick — is tied to your starting choice. The other nine are available regardless of which protagonist you select, giving you a broad pool to explore across a full playthrough.
