- **Talk every day.** Even a one-line greeting keeps affection from decaying.
- **Learn three favorites per candidate.** You rarely need more than that early on.
- **Walk the whole town weekly.** New NPCs and requests appear on schedules you cannot guess.

If you are planning a long save file, treat the first season as reconnaissance. Meet everyone, log their locations and schedules, and hold off on big commitments until you have a clear picture of the whole town.
