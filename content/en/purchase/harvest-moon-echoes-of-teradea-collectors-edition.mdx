## Red Flags: Protecting Your Money

Scams around limited editions are common, and they get more creative as launch approaches.

| Red Flag | What It Usually Means | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Payment by gift card or crypto | No buyer protection | Walk away |
| "Guaranteed" unannounced contents | Speculative listing | Wait for official confirmation |
| Price far below MSRP | Counterfeit or bait listing | Verify the seller's history |
| No cancellation policy stated | Difficult refunds | Ask before paying |
| Pressure to buy "in the next hour" | Artificial urgency | Slow down; real listings last days |

Also be careful with "sealed" resale listings. A sealed box is only valuable if the seal is genuine, and that's difficult to verify from photos alone. For high-value purchases, ask for the original order receipt.
